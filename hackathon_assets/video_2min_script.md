## 🎯 Pro Tips for Recording Your 2-Minute Video

1. **Audio Quality**: Use a clear microphone or headset with minimal background noise.
2. **Cursor Speed**: Move your mouse smoothly and deliberately when clicking tabs (`🏢 SF Custom Fields Table`, `⚡ Execute Agent Workflows`).
3. **Resolution**: Record at **1920x1080 (1080p)** in full screen mode for maximum visual polish.
4. **Software Recommendation**: Use **OBS Studio** (Free) or **Loom** for seamless screen + voiceover recording.
